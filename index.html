What Is Reverb in Music?

“Every sound that you hear is informed by the space that it is heard in.”

Reverb happens when sound hits a surface and reflects back at varying amplitudes and times, creating a complex echo that carries information across physical space. Without it, sound often feels thin or unnatural ‐ which is exactly why clean source recording matters before a single plugin gets touched.
